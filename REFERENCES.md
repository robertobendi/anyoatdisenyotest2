# REFERENCES

## Overview
Both references are warm, editorial websites for architecture / interior-design
studios — cream grounds, serif-display headings, clean sans body, large
photography of warm timber-and-arch interiors, and stats set like an editorial
spec sheet. They form a single coherent direction. **ref-00 (TerraForma) is the
primary reference**: it is the exact business category (a sustainable
*architecture* firm) and its green-plus-warm-wood palette aligns directly with
Anyo at Disenyo's olive monogram. **ref-01 (OURA & CO.) is secondary**: it
informs specific moves — the bold uppercase photo-overlay hero, the large
editorial stat numbers, and the "GET YOUR BRIEF" primary-CTA pattern. One
direction must lead; lead with ref-00's calm editorial system and borrow
ref-01's confident hero and stat treatment.

## ref-00.png

### Typography
Headings are a **transitional / modern serif** with moderate thick-thin
contrast and an editorial, almost magazine feel — close to **Tiempos** or
**Newsreader** (Google Fonts analog: **Newsreader** or **Source Serif 4**;
**Fraunces** if more character is wanted). Body, eyebrows, and the small
service labels are a **neutral humanist/grotesque sans**, close to **Inter /
Söhne** (analog: **Inter** or **Manrope**). Weight contrast is gentle — serif
headings sit at a regular-to-medium weight (not heavy display), body is light.
Tracking is normal; small UPPERCASE eyebrow labels carry slightly expanded
tracking. Case is mixed-case for headings and body, UPPERCASE for tiny labels.
Two faces: serif display + neutral sans.

### Palette
`#f1ede4 — warm cream — page surface`
`#e7e1d4 — oat / pale linen — secondary panels`
`#33402f — deep forest green — ink accent / logo & headings`
`#5d6b4a — sage olive — secondary green`
`#b9722f — burnt amber — warm timber tone from the hero ceiling photo`
`#d99a52 — honey amber — highlight in the wood photography`
`#1d1c19 — near-black bark — body ink`

### Layout signature
Calm multi-column editorial grid. Opens with a small left-aligned intro
paragraph and a small inset landscape photo top-right (restrained, not a
full-bleed hero). A serif section title ("Crafting Sustainable Solutions…")
introduces a clean three-column service row ("Sustainable Architecture
Design / Green Building Consultation / Project Planning & Development").
Mid-page a **single full-bleed photographic band** (a warm wooden vaulted
ceiling shot up its centerline) carries an overlaid serif headline "Build
Your Sustainable Space" — the one big visual beat. Closes with a
two-column block pairing a heading against a right-aligned spec/stat table.
The recognisable move: **generous cream negative space punctuated by one
warm full-bleed timber photograph**, everything else respecting the gutters.

### Imagery treatment
Architectural and environmental photography — built timber structures,
landscape, interiors. Warm, natural grading with golden wood tones; not
blown-out, gentle contrast. Crops are confident: one tight symmetrical
up-the-centerline shot of a wooden vault for the hero, smaller rectangular
environmental insets elsewhere. Full-bleed for the hero band, neatly framed
rectangles otherwise. No filters or gimmicks — clean and natural.

### Decoration & texture
Minimal. Thin hairline rules separating sections, small UPPERCASE eyebrow
labels, and an editorial spec/stat table. No stickers, badges, or
ornament — restraint is the point. Texture comes only from the warm wood in
the photography against flat cream paper.

### Motion / texture cues
Reads as a quiet, print-still editorial site — implied subtle fades and a
slow, calm scroll rhythm; no marquee, no bounce. Any motion should be
understated (soft reveal on scroll, gentle image parallax at most).

### One-line mood
"Sunlit timber-frame monograph — Kinfolk meets a sustainable architecture studio."

## ref-01.png

### Typography
Two distinct voices. The hero and big statements use a **heavy condensed-ish
uppercase grotesque** — bold, tight-tracked sans set in all caps ("WE DESIGN
THE FEELING OF YOU"), close to **Söhne Kräftig / GT America** or, for more
punch, **Archivo Black / Anton** (Google analog: **Archivo** heavy /
**Manrope** ExtraBold; **Anton** if a true condensed display is wanted). The
wordmark and editorial body lean on an **elegant high-contrast serif**
("OURA & CO.," and the manifesto line) — close to **Tiempos / Editorial New**
(analog: **Fraunces** or **Newsreader**). Strong weight contrast: heavy
uppercase display vs light serif body. Tracking on the hero is tight; eyebrow
labels are small UPPERCASE. Pairing: heavy grotesque display + refined serif.

### Palette
`#eae3d8 — warm beige — page surface`
`#e0d7c8 — sand — secondary panels`
`#3a2a20 — espresso brown — dark stat/CTA card`
`#8a6a4d — warm walnut — interior photography midtone`
`#b79c7d — tan / camel — warm accent`
`#1a1714 — near-black — ink`
`#ffffff — white — knock-out type over the hero photo`

### Layout signature
Studio-site grid with a real top nav (wordmark left, menu center, **"GET YOUR
BRIEF"** button right). Opens on a **full-bleed warm interior photograph**
(arched, timber, low-lit) with a huge uppercase headline knocked out over it
and a CTA button. Below, an asymmetric editorial section: a large serif
brand restatement and manifesto paragraph on the left, and a column of
**oversized editorial stat numbers** on the right ("4 years," "20 designers,"
"62 completed projects"), with one **dark espresso card** breaking the grid as
a callout. A small warm still-life (ceramics) anchors the lower left. The
recognisable move: **giant uppercase type laid directly over a warm
full-bleed interior, then big numbers as proof.**

### Imagery treatment
Environmental interior photography — arches, wood, furniture, warm low light —
plus an intimate still-life of ceramics. Warm grading, slightly moody and
low-contrast, rich browns; a hint of motion blur in the hero figure gives it
a lived-in, cinematic feel. Crops are wide and architectural for the hero,
tight and tactile for the still-life. Hero is full-bleed with white overlaid
type; supporting images are framed rectangles.

### Decoration & texture
Sparse and intentional: one **dark espresso callout card** breaking the grid,
thin rules, small UPPERCASE eyebrow tags, oversized numerals as graphic
elements, and a pill-shaped CTA button. No stickers or kitsch — the
"decoration" is typographic scale and the single dark card.

### Motion / texture cues
Expects subtle, premium motion — a slow hero image drift / faint motion blur,
snappy but soft fades, gentle hover states on nav and the CTA. Cinematic and
calm, never bouncy.

### One-line mood
"Warm-minimal interior studio at golden hour — espresso, oak, and one very
confident headline."
