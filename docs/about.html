The practice

Form, Then Design

Anyo at Disenyo

anyo at disenyo — form and design. Ibig sabihin: anyo at disenyo. We're a small, owner-led architectural practice in Batangas City that believes a good home begins with listening — to your family, your lot, and the way the light falls — long before the first line is drawn.

The people behind it

A licensed architect, on every plan

Anyo at Disenyo is led by a PRC-licensed architect who works directly with each family — no handing your project down a chain of juniors. That means the person who hears your idea is the same one who draws it and checks the build.

We're rooted here in Batangas, and we plan to be for a long time. Most of our work comes from neighbours and the families they refer — which is exactly the kind of practice we set out to be.
