## 5 · Design

### Direction
Apply ref-01's confident photo-poster studio system to ANYO at DISENYO: every page opens on a warm, low-lit, full-bleed architectural photograph carrying a giant Anton uppercase headline knocked out in paper-white, immediately followed by proof — oversized stat numerals and one espresso-black card breaking the grid. The calm comes from ref-00's cream gutters, hairline rules, and Fraunces serif restatement, so the boldness reads as a credible, established local practice rather than a loud agency. Touchstones: OURA & CO.'s golden-hour knockout hero and 62/20 stat numerals, re-keyed onto TerraForma's warm-cream editorial paper and the firm's brand-locked olive moss.

### Layout signature
Every page hero is a full-bleed warm photograph with a giant Anton uppercase headline (clamp up to 7.5rem, line-height 0.92, tight letter-spacing −0.01em) knocked out in paper-white #f6f2ea bleeding edge-to-edge of the viewport, and the proof section that follows pins oversized stat numerals (clamp up to 9rem Anton) against the gutter while ONE espresso-black #2a2620 card breaks the grid by overlapping into the column to its left — this knockout-hero-then-grid-breaking-numerals beat repeats on every page.

### Type scale
- Display (hero h1, Anton): `clamp(3.25rem, 9vw, 7.5rem)` / line-height 0.92 / weight 400 (Anton reads ≈700)
- Stat numerals (Anton, graphic): `clamp(4rem, 10vw, 9rem)` / line-height 0.85
- H1 (Anton or Fraunces): `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4rem)`
- H2 (Anton): `clamp(2rem, 3.5vw, 2.75rem)`
- H3 (Fraunces 400): 1.375rem
- Body (Inter 400): 1rem / line-height 1.6
- Manifesto (Fraunces 400): `clamp(1.375rem, 2.5vw, 1.75rem)` / line-height 1.4
- Small / caption / eyebrow (Inter 600): 0.8125rem / `letter-spacing: 0.10em` / UPPERCASE
Scale ratio ≈1.5; weights loaded: Anton 400, Inter 400 + 600, Fraunces 400.

### Spacing & rhythm
- Container max-width: **1180px** (generous), with hero photo bands and the mid-page photo band going **full-bleed** outside it.
- Section vertical padding: small **3rem**, large **7rem** (hero bands min-height 78vh).
- Grid gutters: **2rem** desktop, 1.25rem mobile; 12-column reference grid.
- Baseline grid: no strict baseline grid; instead a consistent 8px spacing unit governs vertical rhythm so the editorial calm holds without rigidity.

### Decorative system
- Oversized Anton stat numerals — proof row directly below the hero on Home and About, used as graphic elements not just data.
- Single espresso-black callout card breaking the grid — the conversion prompt on Home, Services, and Contact.
- Moss-green pill CTA button — in the top nav, the hero, and section closers.
- UPPERCASE eyebrow tags (Inter 600, brand taupe #6c645b, +0.10em) — above every H2 on every page.
- Full-bleed knockout-type photo band — one mid-page on Home and Work, with an overlaid Fraunces caption.
- Thin hairline rules (1px, walnut #8a6a4d at low opacity) — separating major sections.

### Components
- **Header / nav**: Slim sticky bar on warm beige; cropped "ad" monogram left, four mixed-case Inter links center-right, moss-green pill "Get Your Brief" far right. On hero pages the bar sits transparent over the photo with paper-white links, flipping to dark ink on scroll.
- **Hero**: Full-bleed warm photo, min-height ~78vh, Anton headline knocked out in #f6f2ea bottom-left, short Inter subhead beneath, moss pill CTA; subtle dark gradient scrim for legibility.
- **Content section**: Cream ground, eyebrow tag + Anton/Fraunces heading, generous gutters, hairline rule above; multi-column where editorial (3-col services, 2-col closers).
- **Card**: Two flavors — light sand #e0d7c8 framed cards for projects/services (square photo top, caption block), and the single espresso-black #2a2620 callout card with paper-white type + moss CTA that overlaps/breaks the grid.
- **Footer**: Espresso-black or deep-walnut ground, full logo lock-up large, contact block, Facebook link, secondary nav, PRC line — the one consistently dark anchor of the site.

- **Forms**: Inter labels as small UPPERCASE eyebrows above inputs; inputs are underline-only (1px walnut bottom border, no box) on cream; submit = moss pill "Get Your Brief"; espresso contact card beside it.

### Motion
CSS-only, premium and calm per ref-01. Hero photos get a slow 8–12s ease drift (subtle scale 1.0→1.05 transform) and section content fades/translates up ~16px on scroll via a single IntersectionObserver-driven `.in-view` class; the moss pill and nav links get soft 150ms color/opacity hover transitions; the espresso card lifts 4px on hover. No marquee, no bounce, no parallax beyond the gentle hero drift. The only `<script>` is a tiny vanilla nav toggle for mobile and the IntersectionObserver reveal — nothing more.

### What NOT to do
- Do NOT center the hero headline on a white card over generic stock — the headline must be Anton uppercase knocked out paper-white over a FULL-BLEED warm photo, bottom-left.
- Do NOT render stats as small inline figures in cards — they must be oversized Anton numerals (≥4rem) functioning as graphic elements against the gutter.
- Do NOT pair Inter + Lora, use a 3-card home page, or rely on `border-radius: 8px` as the only decoration; the espresso grid-breaking card and full-bleed bands are mandatory ornament.
- Do NOT use pure #ffffff backgrounds, tech-blue, brutalist, or Y2K motifs — surface is warm beige #ece5da and accents are brand-locked moss/olive/taupe.
- Do NOT redraw the logo as a generic text wordmark or recolor the moss symbol away from #6e7044 — keep and place the real og-image lock-up.
